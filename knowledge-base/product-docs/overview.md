# VibeAgent Product Capabilities

## Overview
VibeAgent delivers end-to-end marketing automation across three core operational pillars:

### 1. Autonomous Content Generation
- Multi-channel post creation (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Threads, Instagram).
- Grounded RAG integration with verified company documentation and case studies.
- Automatic hashtag optimization, hook scoring, and tone adaptation.

### 2. Multi-Channel Engagement & Sentiment Analysis
- Real-time ingestion of comments, mentions, and direct messages.
- Zero-shot sentiment polarity and intent detection.
- Contextual reply drafting with human review escalation for sensitive interactions.

### 3. Lead Qualification & CRM Sync
- 5-stage qualification pipeline (`cold`, `warm`, `hot`, `mql`, `sql`).
- Dynamic numerical scoring (0-100) based on budget, timeline, authority, and engagement velocity.
- Bi-directional webhook synchronization with HubSpot, Salesforce, and custom endpoints.
