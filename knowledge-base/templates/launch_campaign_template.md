# B2B Product Launch Campaign Playbook

## Overview
This playbook guides the marketing and content agents during major feature announcements and product launches.

## Tone & Style Guidelines
- **Opening Hook**: Bold statement, surprising metric, or direct question highlighting a common operational headache.
- **Body**: 3 concise bullet points with emojis highlighting the problem, the solution, and the measurable business outcome.
- **Call to Action (CTA)**: Invite comments, request beta access, or link to documentation.
- **Hashtags**: Include 3 to 5 targeted tags (e.g., `#B2BMarketing`, `#AIAutomation`, `#SaaSGrowth`).
