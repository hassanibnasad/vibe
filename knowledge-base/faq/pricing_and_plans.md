# Pricing & Plans FAQ

## 1. What are the available tiers for VibeAgent?
VibeAgent provides three transparent pricing tiers:
- **Starter Tier ($49/month)**: Up to 5 social channels, automated scheduling, standard 8B local LLM inference, and up to 500 AI replies per month.
- **Growth Tier ($199/month)**: Up to 20 social channels, advanced BANT lead scoring, multi-variant A/B content generation, RAG knowledge base integration, and 2,500 AI replies per month.
- **Enterprise Tier (Custom / Self-Hosted)**: Unlimited platforms, dedicated GPU cluster routing (70B models), custom CRM connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot), full data sovereignty, and SLA-backed 24/7 support.

## 2. Does VibeAgent offer a free trial?
Yes, a 14-day full-access trial is available for all cloud and self-hosted instances with no credit card required.

## 3. Can I bring my own OpenAI / Anthropic / local Ollama models?
Absolutely. VibeAgent is 100% open-source and modular. You can connect your self-hosted Ollama or vLLM instance or configure proprietary model endpoints.
