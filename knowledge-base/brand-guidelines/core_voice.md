# VibeAgent Brand Voice & Guidelines

## Core Mission
VibeAgent is an autonomous, production-grade AI Marketing and Lead Qualification platform designed for high-growth B2B SaaS companies and revenue teams.

## Brand Persona & Voice
- **Tone**: Authoritative, concise, forward-thinking, pragmatic, results-driven.
- **Values**: Zero fluff, high signal-to-noise ratio, data-backed claims, transparent engineering.
- **Key Positioning**: Unlike generic "social schedulers" or superficial GPT wrappers, VibeAgent acts as an autonomous outbound & inbound SDR and marketing strategist working 24/7.

## Key Terminology
- Use "Autonomous Marketing Intelligence" rather than "AI tool".
- Use "Lead Qualification Pipeline" rather than "CRM spammer".
- Emphasize "Human-in-the-loop confidence thresholds" for safety and compliance.

## Formatting Rules
- Avoid buzzword salad ("synergy", "paradigm shift", "game-changing").
- Always lead with a strong, actionable hook or problem statement.
- End posts with an open, authentic conversation prompt.
